# GAPS

Everything missing, contradictory or unverifiable found while restructuring the `@kiralygyula92/react-pdf-viewer` site onto PPDS v1.0 (`docs/ppds/02-plugin-docs-standard.md`).

Rules (brief §0.4): no gap is closed by inventing data. Every item stays **Unreviewed** until a human marks it _Accepted_, _Resolved_ (with the commit or decision) or _Exception_ (then recorded in `EXCEPTIONS.md`).

Opened in Phase 1 (Audit), 2026-09-15, at commit `f39ecdb`. Artefacts: `audit/pages.csv`, `audit/capabilities.md`, `audit/assets.csv`.

Severity: **Blocker** stops a later phase · **Major** breaks a MUST in the standard · **Minor** is a quality or accuracy issue.
