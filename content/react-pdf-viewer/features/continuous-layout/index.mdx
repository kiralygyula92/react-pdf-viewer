## Basics

`layout="continuous"` shows all pages in a vertical, scrollable column.

<Demo id="features/continuous-layout/demo-basics" title="A 40-page document, scrolling" />

Every page reserves its space immediately, so the scrollbar is accurate from the start, but only the visible pages and one page on each side are rendered to a canvas. The page indicator follows the scroll position, and previous and next scroll to the neighbouring page.

### With thumbnails and page input

Continuous layout pairs well with [thumbnails](/react-pdf-viewer/thumbnails/) and the [page number input](/react-pdf-viewer/page-number-input/) for long documents.

## Advanced / escape hatch

For layouts the viewer does not offer, such as two pages side by side, render pages yourself with [`usePdfDocument` and `PdfPageCanvas`](/react-pdf-viewer/headless/).

## Limitations

- **No spreads.** Pages are always shown one per row. Workaround: a [headless](/react-pdf-viewer/headless/) layout that places two `PdfPageCanvas` elements per row.
- **Scrolling fast shows blank pages briefly.** Pages outside the rendered window paint when they come into view. Workaround: none needed for most documents; very slow devices benefit from a lower `maxCanvasPixels`.
- **Page sizes are read when the document opens.** Every page's size is measured so the scrollbar is accurate, which takes a moment on documents with thousands of pages; pages render as usual meanwhile.
- **Search and links render per visible page.** Highlights and link areas appear as pages render, which is expected.
