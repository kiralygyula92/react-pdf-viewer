## Basics

`renderToolbar(api)` replaces the toolbar with anything you render, driven by the [viewer API](/react-pdf-viewer/imperative-api/).

<Demo id="features/custom-toolbar/demo-basics" title="Your own controls" />

The custom toolbar sits where the built-in one would, honouring `toolbar.position`. It re-renders with a fresh `api` whenever page, zoom, rotation or status change.

### Reusing the built-in toolbar

`PdfToolbar` is the built-in toolbar as a component. Render it with a subset of actions next to your own buttons.

<Demo
  id="features/custom-toolbar/demo-reuse"
  title="Built-in navigation plus a custom print button"
/>

`PdfToolbar` keeps its accessibility (single tab stop, arrow-key navigation, the responsive **More actions** menu) wherever you place it.

## Customization

`PdfToolbar` takes `config`, `labels`, `locale`, `compact`, `zoomReset`, a replacement `pageIndicator` and `menuPlacement`.

<Demo
  id="features/custom-toolbar/demo-customization"
  title="A wider toolbar with a custom page indicator"
/>

Every prop is listed in the [`PdfToolbarProps` reference](/react-pdf-viewer/api/pdf-toolbar-props/); see also the [customization guide](/react-pdf-viewer/customization/).

## Advanced / escape hatch

Hide the toolbar (`toolbar={false}`) and render controls anywhere in your layout with a [ref](/react-pdf-viewer/imperative-api/), or build a viewer from scratch with the [headless](/react-pdf-viewer/headless/) pieces.

<Callout type="warning" title="Accessibility is yours in a custom toolbar">
  A `renderToolbar` UI replaces the built-in keyboard and screen reader behaviour. Label every
  control, keep disabled states in sync with `canZoomIn`, `canZoomOut`, `page` and `numPages`, and
  announce page changes, as the first demo does with `aria-live`.
</Callout>

## Limitations

- **One toolbar slot.** `renderToolbar` renders in the toolbar position only. Workaround: hide the toolbar and place controls anywhere with a ref.
- **`PdfToolbar` needs a viewer API.** It cannot drive a headless `PdfPageCanvas` directly. Workaround: build headless controls from `usePdfDocument` state.
