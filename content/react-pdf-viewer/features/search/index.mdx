## Basics

`search` adds a find bar that searches every page and highlights the matches.

<Demo id="features/search/demo-basics" title="Search a 40-page document" />

Matching is case-insensitive. <kbd>Enter</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> step to the next and previous match, moving to its page and scrolling it into view; <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the field. With focus inside the viewer, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> focuses the search field. The result count (“2 of 5”) is announced as it changes. `search` implies the [text layer](/react-pdf-viewer/text-layer/).

### With continuous layout

In [continuous layout](/react-pdf-viewer/continuous-layout/), stepping through matches scrolls the document to each one.

<Demo id="features/search/demo-continuous" title="Search with continuous layout and thumbnails" />

## Customization

Highlight colours and the search bar surface are CSS variables; the field's name and result texts are labels.

<Demo id="features/search/demo-customization" title="Blue highlights and shorter result labels" />

See `--rpv-search-highlight`, `--rpv-search-highlight-selected`, `--rpv-search-bg` and `--rpv-search-fg` in the [CSS variables reference](/react-pdf-viewer/api/css-variables/) and the [customization guide](/react-pdf-viewer/customization/).

## Advanced / escape hatch

[`PdfPageCanvas`](/react-pdf-viewer/headless/) accepts a `highlight` (`{ query, selected }`, see [`PageHighlight`](/react-pdf-viewer/api/page-highlight/)) and reports the selected match's element through `onHighlight`, so a custom UI can drive search itself.

## Limitations

- **Plain text matching.** No regular expressions, whole-word or case-sensitive options. Workaround: extract text with `getDocument()` and PDF.js `getTextContent()`, search it yourself, and highlight with `PdfPageCanvas`.
- **Text split across lines is not matched as one phrase.** A phrase broken by a line or column break may not be found. Workaround: search for a shorter part of the phrase.
- **Scanned pages cannot be searched.** Image-only pages contain no text; see [Text layer](/react-pdf-viewer/text-layer/).
